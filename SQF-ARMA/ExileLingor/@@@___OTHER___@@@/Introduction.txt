**Tripwire Gaming Community - Exile Lingor PvP**

A fresh new Exile server with a lot of additional scripts and addons, we're one of the few Lingor Exile servers. 
The server is built with PvP in mind and is balanced around progression.
You start off with access to basic weapons and gear in traders. Nothing is free and high caliber weapons are rare.
Development of the server is still in an early stage but we have quite a lot of features already with a lot more additions coming soon.

Starting a server is one of the hardest things to do, especially in a 7 year old game with a small playerbase but we're here to stick around.
Player count will be low in the beginning and we're not sure if there's an interest for the Exile experience on the not so well known lingor map.
Early players do have an advantage over the others though :wink:

Our server has all the usual stuff like DMS Missions, Capture Points, Zombies and Hordes.
